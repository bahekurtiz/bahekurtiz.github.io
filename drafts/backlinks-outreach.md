# Backlinks – white-hat outreach list (owner-ready, 27 Nov 2026)

Rule: only real listings, real stories and real guides. **No paid links, no link exchanges, no PBNs, no "guest post for $".**
Every pitch offers something useful (photos, facts from our Sanganer unit, a free guide) – the link is the credit, not the price.
Before sending, open each site and check it is still active and takes contributions. Track replies in the table at the end.

## 4. Journalists (free expert quotes)

Sign up as a source on Qwoted and Featured.com (free tiers). Answer only questions about Indian ethnic wear, block printing, sizing for South Asian outfits, or wedding guest dressing – as the founder, with a real name.

## 5. Things NOT to do
- Buying links, "sponsored guest posts", link farms, comment spam, forum signature links.
- Asking for a link in exchange for a discount or free product without the blogger disclosing it (that needs a clear "gifted" label and a `rel="sponsored"` link).
- Copying our own blog posts onto other sites (duplicate content).
