FIXED IN THIS RELEASE

3. Mobile menu keeps a manual language switch as a fallback.
4. Cloudflare edge country detection endpoint (/api/geo) feeds automatic country selection.
5. Automatic language follows country + browser preference where a country is multilingual (e.g. Canada English/French).
6. Manual language choice persists and overrides automatic language.
7. Product title + description translations are wired for EN/HI/FR/DE/ES/IT/JA/AR for the current four products.
8. Arabic RTL remains supported.
9. No full-body language MutationObserver was added.
10. Desktop has direct Discover access to Watch & Shop, Mirror, Wishlist, Share & Earn, My Designs and Gift Card, matching mobile feature reachability.
11. Mirror performance: MediaPipe pose AI is bounded and runs on the shopper photo; product dress layers use a lightweight crop path instead of pose analysis on every product.
12. INR -> USD automatic selling-price fallback remains removed.
13. International storefront is enabled even when a product has no international price; missing prices show as request/availability rather than inventing a selling price.
14. Per-country explicit price architecture is included through product.market_prices.
15. Existing India Razorpay server-side create/verify/status foundation is preserved.
